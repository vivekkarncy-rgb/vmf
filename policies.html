Corporate policies, without the ambiguity.

Have a question about our credit parameters, loan terms, or underwriting guidelines? Reach our compliance desk directly at compliance@vmffinance.com or visit our Hyderabad office.

Cash-Flow & Transaction Discipline: A business's ability to generate cash flow, fulfil orders, collect receivables and meet repayment obligations is central to a sound credit assessment. VMF evaluates relevant commercial transactions, financial performance, banking conduct, buyer relationships and other applicable credit parameters.
Multi-Factor Evaluation Criteria: We assess genuine commercial delivery proof, debtor creditworthiness, 12-month banking conduct, and cash-flow turnaround speed rather than relying solely on arbitrary physical real-estate collateral.
*Security, personal/corporate guarantees, receivable assignment and other credit support may be required depending on the facility structure. Every financing proposal is subject to due diligence, eligibility criteria, credit sanction, and mutually agreed documentation.
Transparent Sanction Terms Mandate: Prior to executing any credit arrangement, every eligible borrower receives an individualized sanction schedule disclosing the sanctioned limit, effective Annual Percentage Rate (APR), itemized fees, tenure, and transparent repayment schedules. There are zero hidden costs.
Non-Discriminatory & Transparent Lending: VMF does not discriminate on grounds of gender, caste, religion, or community. Loan terms and communications are provided in clear, understandable language.
Ethical Recovery Standards: All collections and client communications are conducted strictly during standard business hours (08:00 to 19:00). We adhere to non-coercive recovery practices that strictly respect borrower dignity and personal privacy.
Risk-Graduated Pricing: Interest rates are determined using a disciplined risk-based pricing framework incorporating cost of funds, borrower risk rating, transaction liquidity, and underlying debtor credit enhancement.

Fair Lending Regulatory Compliance: In strict compliance with RBI circulars on Fair Lending Practice, penalty for delayed payment is levied strictly as 'penal charges' and not as compounded penal interest added to the loan balance.

Customer Due Diligence (CDD) Standards: VMF complies with the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), 2002 and RBI KYC Master Directions. Prior to credit sanction, all applicants must submit verified business registration (Certificate of Incorporation / Partnership Deed / Udyam Certificate), PAN, GSTIN, registered address proof, and identify Natural Persons qualifying as Ultimate Beneficial Owners (UBO).
Ongoing Transaction Monitoring: Credit facilities are disbursed and repaid solely through verifiable institutional banking channels to prevent financial fraud and ensure complete commercial authenticity.
Confidentiality of Commercial Information: We understand that trade invoices, supplier contracts, purchase orders, buyer lists, and bank statements represent sensitive commercial property. VMF maintains rigorous physical, technical, and operational safeguards to protect customer data.
Restricted Information Sharing: Borrower financial records are accessed strictly for credit appraisal, facility servicing, statutory reporting to authorized Credit Information Companies (CIBIL, Experian, CRIF High Mark, Equifax), and compliance with judicial or regulatory authorities under applicable laws.
