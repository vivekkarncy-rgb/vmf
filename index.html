Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, straightforward answers about our MSME financing facilities, eligibility, and process.

We offer Invoice Financing, Purchase Order Financing, and Working Capital Loans to help eligible businesses manage cash flow, fulfil orders, and meet daily expenses.

MSMEs across various sectors can apply. Eligibility depends on your business profile, financial performance, banking history, repayment capacity, and applicable credit policies.

Pricing and charges depend on the facility and assessed credit risk. Applicable interest, fees, APR, and repayment terms are disclosed in the sanction documents and Key Facts Statement, wherever applicable.

Documents may include business KYC, financial statements, bank statements, and GST information. Depending on the product, purchase orders, invoices, delivery evidence, and buyer confirmations may also be required.

Repayment follows the schedule and collection arrangement agreed for your facility. These details are provided in the loan documents before you accept the terms.
